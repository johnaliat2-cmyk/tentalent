Proposal Criteria

What to include in your submission

We appreciate thoughtful brevity over lengthy pitch decks. Ensure your proposal touches upon:

  • check
    Verifiable demand & real customers

    Evidence of authentic need, recurring engagement, or organic word-of-mouth validation.

  • check
    Unit economics & balance sheet integrity

    Clean cost structures, gross margins, and manageable liabilities without speculative debt.

  • check
    Operational stewardship & governance

    How you lead, honor team members, suppliers, and environmental or civic commitments.

  • check
    12-month capital deployment milestones

    What the capital achieves, non-dilutive milestones, and runway clarity.

  • check
    Downside protection & resilience plan

    How the business withstands macro contractions, supply shocks, or pricing pressures.
