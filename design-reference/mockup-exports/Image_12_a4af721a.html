Letter from the Foundation

Why Ten Talents was established.

In contemporary finance, patient stewardship has been usurped by dopamine-driven loops, predatory influencers, and high-frequency gamification. Novices are coerced into high-volatility environments under the false guise of simple speed, inevitably absorbing devastating losses before having mastered rudimentary execution mechanics.

We built Ten Talents as an antidote: a quiet harbor where students learn market mechanics rigorously without risking capital, where discipline precedes exposure, and where wealth is understood as a stewardship mandate rather than a speculative shortcut.

“If you cannot steward fifty pounds with calm discipline, fifty thousand will merely accelerate your distress.”

True mastery is slow. It asks for the patient calibration of emotional self-restraint, respect for structural order flows, and the humility to remain a student long after the crowd has abandoned sobriety.

Alistair Vance Founder, Ten Talents Education Ltd
London · Michaelmas Term 2024
