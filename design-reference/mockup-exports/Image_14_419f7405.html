Community Standards & Covenants

House Rules for Every Room.

verified_user Uncompromising Academic Rigour

Our integrity as an educational fellowship relies upon unwavering adherence to scholastic rigor. These covenants are non-negotiable baselines designed to protect your attention, cultivate deep intellectual stamina, and preserve an environment of mutual trust.

1

Market discussion and education only

Strictly non-advisory. Dialogue is solely dedicated to deconstructing market mechanics, liquidity cycles, portfolio construction theory, and long-term economic frameworks.

2

No signals, no tips

Absolutely no buy/sell alerts, copy trading invitations, trade calls, or predictive advice. Impulsive behavior is analyzed as a psychological pitfall, never encouraged.

3

No referral or broker links

Zero affiliate schemes, commercial promotions, sponsored products, or external brokerage recruitment. The community is completely untainted by commercial incentives.

4

Groups named ‘Ten Talents — [Topic]’

All authentic community channels carry our standard institutional nomenclature. If an external group is not named strictly “Ten Talents — [Topic]”, it is not affiliated with our foundation.

Enforcement Protocol

Violations result in immediate removal without warning to preserve the sanctity of our members' focus.
