Codex Section 01

Terms of Use & Academic Scope

Academic Fellowship Compact

“Ten Talents exists solely to cultivate financial literacy, intellectual discernment, and macro discipline. Participation in our modules implies formal acceptance that all materials constitute scholarly education, not individualized financial counsel.”

1.1 Non-Advisory Nature of Fellowship

All lectures, analytical essays, white papers, mathematical models, syllabus repositories, and interactive webinars delivered under the Ten Talents brand are structured exclusively for educational enlightenment and historical case review. Under no circumstances should any syllabi, simulation figures, or scholarly exchanges be construed as personalized investment advice, wealth structuring, tax counseling, or legal directives.

1.2 Academic Material License & Intellectual Property

Fellows are granted a revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable personal license to review curriculum materials. Commercial syndication, redistribution, automated scraping via crawlers, LLM model ingestion, or public reproduction of proprietary frameworks without explicit prior written authorization from the Secretariat is strictly prohibited under the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988.

1.3 Integrity of Academic Dialogue & Conduct

Our cohorts are predicated on collegiate rigor and mutual respect. Fellows agree not to post unverified market rumors, coordinate trading actions, solicit commercial counterparties, or deploy speculative hype. Breach of this standard results in instantaneous expulsion and forfeiture of academic privileges without restitution.

Codex Section 02

Privacy Policy & UK GDPR Notice

Ten Talents Education Ltd acts as the Data Controller in accordance with the Data Protection Act 2018 and the United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR). We hold our participants' privacy in the highest institutional regard.

database Information Retained

Academic enrollment records, attendance logs, verified billing references via PCI-compliant processors, and voluntary correspondence.

policy Zero-Sale Guarantee

We never monetize, exchange, or lease participant telemetry or personal identifiers to third-party ad brokers, underwriters, or data houses.

2.1 Lawful Basis for Processing

We process data predominantly under the lawful basis of contractual fulfillment (maintaining fellowship access, delivering course materials) and legitimate interests (maintaining platform integrity, scholarly progress audits, and statutory financial accounting).

2.2 Data Subject Prerogatives

As an individual in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, you hold the statutory right to request access to personal data held, demand rectification of erroneous entries, request erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”), or restrict processing by contacting our Data Protection Officer at dpo@tentalents.co.uk.

2.3 Information Retention Schedule

Personal identifiers are retained throughout the active tenure of your educational membership and archived for precisely 6 calendar years following contract termination to satisfy UK HMRC fiscal compliance standards, after which records undergo cryptographic purging.

Codex Section 04

Capital Risk Disclaimer & FSMA Exemption

warning Statutory Financial Notice: Capital at Risk

The value of public market equities, collective vehicles, sovereign bonds, and alternative asset classes can fluctuate significantly. The value of investments and any income derived can fall as well as rise. Past performance patterns offer no assurance of future trajectories. Capital deployed in financial markets is perpetually exposed to partial or total loss.

4.1 FSMA 2000 Regulatory Demarcation

Ten Talents Education Ltd is an un-regulated educational provider under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (FSMA). We do not conduct regulated investment business, do not hold client money, do not execute broker-dealer orders, and do not hold authorization from the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) to provide individual advice.

4.2 Simulated Laboratory & Paper Portfolios

All computational toolkits, backtests, historical portfolio balances, and simulation sandboxes presented across courses operate strictly in simulated, non-custodial environments without real monetary balances. Theoretical execution results possess innate limitations and fail to capture real market frictions, liquidity gaps, slippage, and emotional pressures.

4.3 Individual Stewardship & Professional Counsel

Before making any personal capital allocation decisions, fellows are explicitly advised to consult an authorized, independent financial adviser (IFA) certified by the Financial Conduct Authority in the United Kingdom or the appropriate sovereign regulatory agency in their home jurisdiction.
